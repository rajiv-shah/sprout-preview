// About

Most groceries are owned by investors. SPRoUT is being built the other way around.

// The shape of the shop

Three commitments we won't compromise on.

01 Structure

Worker-owned. One member, one vote.

Any surplus goes back into wages, into keeping everyday ingredients affordable, and into the long-term health of the business. When we can, some of it will go to community causes we believe in. None of it goes to outside shareholders.

02 The range

Everyday ingredients first. Loose where we can.

Rice, pasta, oats, pulses, potatoes, fresh fruit and veg, core herbs and spices. Much of the range will be loose: weighed out, less packaging, and often cheaper for it. Seasonal produce from growers nearby, paid a fair price.

03 Funding

Funded by people we know and trust.

Opening the shop will require raising capital. While the planning phase is self-funded by the founders, we are looking for values-aligned donations, loans, and investments from individuals and organisations who share our vision, with no strings on how the shop is run.

// From the charter
