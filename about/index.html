// About

 

Most groceries are owned by investors. SPRöUT is being built the other way around.

The people doing the work will own and run the business. We're in the development phase: two founders, a small group of supporters, and a Structural Ethics Charter setting out the principles we're working to. The shop isn't open yet.

// The shape of the shop

Three commitments we won't trade off.

01 Structure

Worker-owned. One member, one vote.

Any surplus goes back into wages, into keeping the staples affordable, and into the long-term health of the business. When we can, some of it will go to community causes we believe in. None of it goes to outside shareholders.

02 The range

Staples first. Loose where we can.

Rice, pasta, oats, pulses, potatoes, fresh fruit and veg, core herbs and spices. Much of the range will be loose: weighed out, less packaging, and often cheaper for it. Seasonal produce from growers nearby, paid a fair price.

03 Funding

Funded by people we know and trust.

SPRöUT will need outside funding to open. We're not seeking open investment. Any funding would come from people or organisations we know and trust, with no say in how the shop is run.

// From the charter

SPRöUT exists to make everyday food less stressful.
