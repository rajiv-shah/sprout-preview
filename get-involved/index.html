// Get Involved

If you'd like to be part of this, here's how.

SPRöUT is being built by a small group of people in Leicester. There are three ways to follow along or take part. None of them ask for money.

01 Newsletter

Sign up for updates

We send an occasional email when there's something worth sharing. Premises news, supplier visits, the next public event when there is one. Pass it on if you know someone who'd be interested.

02 Ownership

Founding worker-member

When the time is right, we'll be looking for founding worker-members: people who want a genuine stake in the project. Sign up to the newsletter to hear when that stage begins.

03 Other ways in

Help us out

If you'd like to help with the project as it develops, write to us. Practical skills, local knowledge, premises leads, supplier contacts. We read everything and reply to everything.

Know of a space?
