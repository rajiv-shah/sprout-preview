Join Us to Help SPRoUT Grow!

We are excited to invite you to our next public meeting on Saturday, September 19th at 2:00 PM at the Friends Meeting House.

Whether you’ve been following our journey from the very beginning or are just curious to learn what a grocery workers' co-operative is all about, this meeting is for you. We are building a low-waste, community-focused wholefoods grocery store that is owned and run by workers, for the benefit of our neighbourhood.

What We'll Cover:

  • The Journey So Far: A look back at what we’ve accomplished over the last few months.
  • Financials & Funding: A high-level overview of our setup budget, financial health, and funding applications.
  • The Property Search: Updates on our search for a safe, accessible location.
  • Spreading the Word: How we are growing our presence in the community.
  • How You Can Get Involved: Concrete ways you can help shape this project.

Guest Speakers

Over the next few months, we will be updating this page with guest speakers who will be joining us to share their insights on co-operative funding, local food networks, and community action. Keep an eye on this space!
