// What's on the shelves

A shop built around staples, weighed loose where we can.

>

Loose pulses, grains, dried fruit — weighed in your own jar or a paper bag. Less packaging, often cheaper than the packaged equivalent.

Fruit & veg from growers nearby, paid a fair price. Seasonal first, supplemented with the staples that aren't.

People behind the counter who know the products, the suppliers, and the price they're sold at.

// What we're building

A plant-based grocery where the staple foods come first.

Rice, pasta, oats, pulses, potatoes, fresh fruit and veg, herbs and spices. Fairly priced and well chosen, with people behind the counter who know the products.

The shop will be worker-owned. The people working in it will own and run it. One member, one vote. Any surplus goes back into wages, into keeping the staples affordable, and into the long-term health of the business.

We're starting with one shop, done properly. Our reference is Unicorn Grocery in Manchester, who've been doing this since 1996.
