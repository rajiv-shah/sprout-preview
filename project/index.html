// Common questions

What people ask us.

What is it?

A worker-owned grocery being developed for Leicester, focused on everyday staples and fair pricing.

When will it open?

No fixed date yet. The site needs to be settled, the financial model finalised, and the founding worker-member group in place first.

Where?

We're taking a detailed look at a site on Abbey Lane in Leicester. Nothing confirmed yet, and we haven't made an offer.

How is it funded?

We're covering the development phase ourselves. Opening the shop will need outside funding, from trusted sources rather than open investors. We'll share more as that takes shape.

Can I invest?

We're not looking for open investment. If you're an individual or organisation we know and trust and you'd like to be involved, get in touch.

How can I support it?

Tell people about it. If you have skills, contacts, or knowledge that could be useful (retail, food, premises, finance), we'd like to hear from you. Drop us a line at ku.oc.yrecorg-tuorps@olleh.
